About

Computer vision engineer and rancher based in Antlers, Oklahoma. I built a production cattle weight estimation system using phone-based computer vision and drone LiDAR, validated against chute data and verified scale weights from my family’s operation.

The work spans model architecture, data pipelines, mobile deployment, and custom drone hardware. I have more than 20 years of cattle operations experience. My daughters now manage day-to-day ranch work while I focus on the technology layer.

I am building a verified identity and value layer for cattle: a long-range ear tag paired with an unremovable bolus, cryptographically bound to each animal and readable from the air. The goal is straightforward — a banker can lend against the animal, an insurer can underwrite it, and a buyer can pay a verified-attribute premium with confidence.

Family ranch, Antlers, Oklahoma — next generation on daily operations; I lead the technology.
